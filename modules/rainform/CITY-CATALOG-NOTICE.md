# City Catalog Attribution

The bundled city-center coordinates and GeoNames identifiers are derived from the GeoNames China country extract (`CN.zip`), downloaded on 2026-10-22 from <https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/CN.zip>.

GeoNames licenses its data under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 License (CC BY 4.0): <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. The source extract README states this license. Feature codes `PPLA` and `PPLA2` identify seats of first- and second-order administrative divisions. The catalog includes matching records in the China extract; the source data is provided as-is and may be incomplete or inaccurate. A Chinese alternate name is used when the extract contains one; otherwise the GeoNames primary name is retained.
